Common questions

How fast is setup?
Done-for-you plans are live within 48 hours of getting access to your accounts. Self-Serve you can set up yourself in under an hour using our Help Center.
Do I need to be technical?
No. On done-for-you plans, we handle everything — connecting accounts, building the AI, setting up automations. You just log in and see your conversations organized.
Is this a chatbot?
No. The AI drafts a reply the second a customer messages. A real human reviews it before sending. Customers always get a personal response — they never know AI helped draft it.
What platforms do you support?
Instagram DMs, Facebook Messenger, and WhatsApp. Email support is available on Growth and above.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. Month-to-month on all plans. No long-term contracts.
What if I want to reply myself sometimes?
You can take over any conversation at any time. Add a "manual" label to a conversation and the AI steps aside — you're always in control.
